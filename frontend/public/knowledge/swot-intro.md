# SWOT Analysis

SWOT analyzes Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats.

## Four Quadrants
- **Strengths**: Internal positive attributes.
- **Weaknesses**: Internal areas for improvement.
- **Opportunities**: External favorable conditions.
- **Threats**: External challenges or risks.

## Best Practices
- Be specific and measurable.
- Link strengths to opportunities.
- Turn weaknesses into learning opportunities.
- SWOT is a map of relationships, not just a list.
- Update regularly as context changes.
