# PESTLE Analysis

PESTLE is a framework for analyzing external factors affecting an organization.

## The Six Factors
1. **Political**: Laws, regulations, government policies.
2. **Economic**: Interest rates, inflation, economic growth.
3. **Social**: Demographics, culture, lifestyle trends.
4. **Technological**: Innovation, R&D, automation.
5. **Legal**: Legislation, compliance requirements.
6. **Environmental**: Climate, sustainability, resources.

## Application
- Use PESTLE to assess market entry, strategy, or risk.
- Link factors together (e.g., how economic affects social).
- Quantify impact where possible (e.g., scale 1–10).
- Consider future trends, not just current state.
